What you'll come away with

🧠Pattern fluency

Recognize two-pointer, sliding-window, hashmap, and DP patterns on sight instead of re-deriving them under pressure.

⚙️Optimization instinct

Always start brute-force, then articulate the exact trade-off that earns the optimal solution.

🚀Complexity precision

🔍Mistake awareness

Know the quiet traps — quadratic .count(), LEFT JOIN collapsing to INNER, WHERE vs HAVING.

⚡DS coding edge

Pandas/NumPy-style manipulation and ML metrics from scratch — the part generic LeetCode prep skips.
